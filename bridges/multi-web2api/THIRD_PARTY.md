The inspected Z.ai frontend selects message version 0/1 from local chat state, rather than a version field in its completion handler. Version 0 applies raw snapshots/edits; version 1 consumes phase deltas and structured `content_blocks`. This decoder supports the shared phase-delta shape and legacy raw mutations. Structured blocks, explicitly versioned response payloads, and ambiguous partial replay of earlier thinking are rejected; phase-local snapshots are not supported. Literal quoting or Markdown fences from thinking cannot change how a later answer is decoded.
